Paul Hollywood Baguette Recipe Modified by HBarreto for use with USA Pan Triple French Baguette Pan and KitchenAid

 

400g King Arthur Bread Flour
8g fast rise yeast
9g salt (supposed to be 8 but I like it saltier)
300ml water, room temp

 

1) Put yeast and salt on top of flour, then drizzle some olive oil over the flour.
Using dough hook, add 250ml and start mixing slow, then one speed faster, then another speed faster.
Add rest of water slowly, while mixing at medium speed for 7 minutes.
Dough is pretty elastic, scrape it out onto floured surface.
Pull, stretch, and fold over several times. Sprinkle flour as needed so not too sticky on fingers and surface.
Form into a smooth ball.
Drizzle some olive oil into the KitchenAid bowl (so dough won’t stick) and drop the ball into the bowl.
Wrap tightly with plastic wrap.
Let rise for 1.5 to 2 hours or so, until a few inches from top of bowl.

 

2) Flour surface and scrape it out of bowl. Pull, stretch and fold several times, handling gently.
Roll it into a tube and cut into three pieces.
Roll each piece into a long, thin tube and place on the pan.
Cover with cloth.
Let rise for 1 to 1.5 hours or so.

 

3) Put a metal pan in the bottom rack of the oven (I use a jelly roll pan) and pre-heat oven to 425 (I use convection bake feature).
Pour at least a quart of hot water onto the hot metal pan to produce steam.
Put the baguettes in the middle rack of the oven for 25 minutes. (You can score them with a utility knife to make them prettier.)
Bake the baguettes in the oven for 25 minutes.

Let cool a few minutes and ready to eat! Store in a bag and reheat in oven (better than toaster) for a few minutes to warm up.
